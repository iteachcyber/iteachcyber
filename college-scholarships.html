College & scholarship resources

Where to look for cybersecurity degree programs, certification-friendly credit, and money to pay for it. Ms. Thomas curates and updates this list — check back each semester.

Choosing a program

Start here before narrowing down schools.

NSA/CISA National Centers of Academic Excellence

A searchable list of colleges formally designated for cybersecurity education — a strong signal of program quality and often tied to scholarship pipelines.

Nevada System of Higher Education (NSHE)

In-state options including UNLV and CSN, both with growing cybersecurity and information technology programs and Nevada-resident tuition.

Credit for Security+

Many colleges award credit or advanced standing for a passed CompTIA Security+ exam — ask an admissions counselor directly what a given school accepts.

Scholarships worth applying for

Cybersecurity-specific funding, updated as new cycles open.

(ISC)² Undergraduate Scholarship

Awarded to students pursuing a cybersecurity-related degree, with awards intended to cover a meaningful share of tuition.

NSA CAE Cyber Scholarship

Scholarship-for-service style funding tied to designated Centers of Academic Excellence, often paired with a post-graduation service commitment.

SANS Women's Academy & CyberStart

Funded training pathways for students who want a head start before their first year of college coursework.

CCSD & Clark County local scholarships

Local Clark County and CCSD Education Foundation scholarships — smaller awards, but often less competitive and stackable with national ones.

FBLA national scholarships

Available to active FBLA members, including awards tied directly to competitive event placement in technology categories.

CyberPatriot & NCL alumni pathways

Several universities offer recruiting consideration or scholarship interviews specifically for competitive CyberPatriot and NCL participants.
