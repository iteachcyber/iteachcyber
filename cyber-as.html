PATHWAY STEP 03 / YEAR 3

Advanced Studies

The pathway's most technical course — built around CompTIA Security+ exam preparation and applied projects that ask students to think like both attacker and defender.

What students learn

  • 01

    Security+ exam preparation

    Structured study through CompTIA CertMaster, aligned chapter by chapter to the current Security+ objectives.

  • 02

    Threats, attacks & vulnerabilities

    A deeper pass on attack techniques and vulnerability classes than Cyber II, with case studies of real breaches.

  • 03

    Governance & non-repudiation

    Policy, accountability, and the legal side of security — including a courtroom-style non-repudiation activity.

  • 04

    Anatomy of a Breach project

    Students research a real-world breach and present it as a physical, hand-drawn poster — no digital design tools, by design.
