GETTING WORK EXPERIENCE

Jobs & internship resources

Real experience is the fastest way to know if this field is for you — and it strengthens every college and scholarship application that follows. Here's where to look.

Start with the program's own network

The lowest-friction way in is through people who already know your work.

Arctiq industry partnership

Sierra Vista's cybersecurity pathway partners directly with Arctiq for mentorship and project exposure — ask Ms. Thomas how current students are getting involved.

CyberPatriot & NCL recruiters

Competitive performance on CyberPatriot or National Cyber League gets noticed — several regional employers actively recruit from top-placing teams.

FBLA employer connections

FBLA state conferences regularly include employer booths and info sessions specifically for technology and information systems students.

Where to search

Entry points built for students, not just experienced professionals.

CISA Cybersecurity Internship Programs

Federal internship pipelines aimed at students, including paid programs designed to run alongside a school year or summer term.

Nevada state & Clark County IT internships

Local government IT departments periodically open internship and student-worker postings — a good option for staying close to home.

Las Vegas tech & casino IT internships

The Las Vegas resort and hospitality industry runs large IT and security operations, and several post entry-level and internship roles each year.

UNLV Cybersecurity Center opportunities

Even before enrolling, UNLV's cybersecurity programs sometimes open workshops, camps, or shadow days to local high school students.

LinkedIn & Handshake

Build a simple profile listing coursework, CompTIA progress, and competition results — recruiters do search for exactly that combination.

CompTIA career resources

CompTIA maintains its own job-board and career-pathway guidance built specifically around its certifications, including Security+.

// build the paper trail

Keep a running record

Save every certificate, competition result, and project — from your first CyberPatriot round to your Security+ CertMaster progress. It becomes your résumé before you ever need to write one.
