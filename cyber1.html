PATHWAY STEP 01 / YEAR 1

Cyber I

The entry point to the pathway. Students build the habits, vocabulary, and technical footing that every later course depends on — no prior experience required.

What students learn

  • 01

    Digital citizenship & safe computing

    Online safety, privacy, acceptable use, and the ethics of working with technology — the ground rules for everything after.

  • 02

    Hardware & operating systems

    How a computer is actually built and how Windows, Linux, and macOS manage files, users, and permissions.

  • 03

    Networking basics

    What a network is, how devices talk to each other, and the vocabulary students will lean on again in Cyber II and AP Networking.

  • 04

    Intro to security mindset

    Early exposure to threats, risk, and defense — enough to make Cyber II feel like a continuation, not a cold start.
