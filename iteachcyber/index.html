About the program

Taught by Rebecca Thomas

Ms. Thomas has spent 20 years in the classroom and leads Sierra Vista's 9–12 Cybersecurity CTE pathway, holding Nevada endorsements in Computer Science, Computer-Based Applications, and School Administration. She teaches all four courses in the pathway, advises the school's FBLA chapter and Cyber Club, and coaches its CyberPatriot and National Cyber League teams.

The curriculum is rebuilt each year against state End-of-Course data, so instructional time goes where students are actually struggling rather than where a textbook assumes they will. The program partners with Arctiq for industry mentorship and real-world project exposure, and its AP Networking pilot has posted a pass rate well above the national average.

The pathway

Four courses, one continuous build toward industry certification.

01 / Year 1

Cyber I

Foundations — digital citizenship, hardware, operating systems, and safe computing habits.

02 / Year 2

Cyber II

Core security concepts — network defense, threat analysis, and hands-on incident response.

03 / Year 3

Advanced Studies

Security+ exam prep through CompTIA CertMaster, plus applied breach and policy projects.

04 / Year 4

AP Networking

College Board's AP Networking pilot — SOHO design, diagnostics, and managing shared connections.
