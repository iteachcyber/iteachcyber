PATHWAY STEP 02 / YEAR 2

Cyber II

Where students move from computing literacy to security practice — network defense, threat identification, and the habits of an incident responder, taught through hands-on labs.

What students learn

  • 01

    Network defense fundamentals

    Firewalls, segmentation, and monitoring — how defenders actually protect a live network, not just describe one.

  • 02

    Threats, attacks & vulnerabilities

    Recognizing common attack patterns and vulnerability classes, and reasoning about how they're exploited and prevented.

  • 03

    Incident response basics

    What to do when something goes wrong — detection, containment, and documentation, practiced through classroom scenarios.

  • 04

    Applied labs & assessment

    Regular hands-on labs paired with EOC-style assessment, so students are ready for the state End-of-Course exam.

Course content is revisited each year against state EOC results, with extra lab time built around the standards students historically find hardest — so the sequence you see reflects real performance data, not just a textbook's table of contents.
