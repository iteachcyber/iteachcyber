AP Networking

The pathway's capstone course, taught as part of College Board's AP Networking pilot. Students design, diagnose, and manage real network connections — the practical skill set that sits underneath everything they've studied so far.

What students learn

  • Unit 1

    Networking fundamentals

    Topics 1.1–1.4: how networks are structured, addressed, and connected — the shared vocabulary for the rest of the course.

  • Unit 2

    Managing My Shared Connections

    Standard 2.1.A — SOHO network diagnostics: setting up, troubleshooting, and securing a small office / home network end to end.

  • Later units

    Building toward the AP exam

    Later units continue to build hands-on diagnostic and design skills, culminating in exam-aligned assessment throughout the year.
